## PART 5 — VAN APP: SIMPLE ENOUGH FOR ANYONE
The current van screens are too complicated. Redesign the **Van app** (and, if role apps from Prompt #13 exist, the same pattern for them) around one idea: **one clear action per screen, big buttons, plain words.** Generate every screen with Stitch (mobile first, 360 px).
1. **Home:** today's load sheet summary, three big buttons: **New Sale · My Stock · End of Day**, today's sales and cash collected vs pending, connection/sync status.
2. **New Sale in 3 steps with a visible stepper:** **1 Customer** (search by name or phone; shows name, phone, address; quick "+ New customer" with just name, mobile, address) → **2 Items** (big product tiles with search; tap opens the unit picker: **Pcs · Carton (Peti) · Dozen · Bag** with a big quantity stepper and a live "12 pcs × ₹x = ₹y" line; running total bar) → **3 Pay** (Cash · UPI · Credit; for Cash and UPI a **Payment Done / Pending** choice, hidden for Credit; Pending needs customer name and mobile) → **Receipt screen** with Print and Share. A back button on every step keeps entered data.
3. **My Stock:** simple list with big numbers (cartons + loose), search, low-stock highlight.
4. **End of Day:** guided steps: stock left → cash to hand over → confirm → summary.
5. Language: short plain words, icons with labels, minimum 16 px text, 48 px tap targets, high contrast, one-hand reachable primary buttons, friendly error messages that say what to do. Add an i18n-ready label layer and an optional **Hindi labels toggle** (P1, only if time remains; staff UI default stays English).
6. If Prompt #13's features already exist (customer details, Pcs/Carton picker, payment Done/Pending, warehouse queue rules), **preserve their behavior exactly**; if they do not exist, **do not build them here** except the unit picker and customer details needed for the 3-step flow, and note it in `docs/memory.md`.
7. **Acceptance (measured):** a scripted new-user run completes a sale in at most the baseline's step count minus 30% (record both), with no step requiring knowledge of system terms; a 10-point heuristic checklist (visibility of status, plain language, error prevention, undo, consistency, etc.) is filled in per screen in `docs/memory.md`.

## PART 7 — KEYBOARD-ONLY BILLING, UNITS AND WEIGHT
1. **Keyboard-only billing (POS, Van counter on desktop/tablet keyboards, Create Order, GRN lines):** after typing an item and quantity, **ArrowDown (or Enter) moves to a new empty row with focus in the product search**; ArrowUp/Down navigate suggestions and rows; Enter selects; Tab moves product → qty → unit → rate; Esc cancels; visible focus; a shortcut help overlay (`?`). A scripted test completes a whole bill with **no mouse**.
2. **Units:** a shared `UnitPicker`/`QtyUnitPicker` offering **Pcs, Carton (pack size), Dozen (12 pcs) and Bag (pieces per bag)**. **Dozen and Bag are offered for products with MRP from ₹1 to ₹100** (threshold configurable); Bag appears when the product has a bag size (admin field, Excel import column, bulk "set bag size" tool; admins see a "set bag size" hint when it is missing). **Values appear automatically:** choosing a unit and quantity instantly shows total pieces, per-pcs rate, line amount and stock check; stock is always deducted in pieces; the invoice stores the unit label and pieces. Same rounding rules as existing pricing.
3. **Product weight:** show the **weight next to the product name** on the Update Stock & Excel Upload screen (for example "Bikaji Achari Masala Mathhi · 200 g") and through one shared `ProductLabel` in POS, Van, GRN and stock lists. Additive nullable fields `weightValue` and `weightUnit` (g, kg, ml, l, pcs). Backfill only as **suggestions with a preview** parsed conservatively from existing names/SKU (for example "50gm", "1 KG", "500ml") that an admin approves in bulk; **never overwrite existing data silently**; the Excel import maps a WEIGHT column. No other change to that screen.
