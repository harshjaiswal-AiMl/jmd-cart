Traditional Stone Chakki Process & Rigorous Cleaning

Aashirvaad Shudh Chakki Atta is made from heavy, golden Sharbati wheat grains sourced directly from MP farmers through ITC's trusted e-Choupal network. The grains undergo a rigorous 4-step mechanized cleaning process to remove dust, stones, husk, and impurities before gentle cold stone chakki milling. This ensures that the essential wheat bran, germ, and natural sweetness are completely preserved, yielding rotis that stay delightfully soft for up to 6 hours.

4-Step Wheat Cleaning Mechanism

  • check_circle 1. Pre-Cleaning & Aspiration: High-powered air suction lifts lightweight chaff, dry stalks, and micro-dust.
  • check_circle 2. De-Stoning & Magnetic Separation: Specific gravity separators and neodymium magnets isolate clay clods, pebbles, and metal shards.
  • check_circle 3. Optical Color Sorting: Advanced bichromatic sensors remove discolored, damaged, or immature grains.
  • check_circle 4. Stone Chakki Milling: Low-temperature stone grinding prevents heat degradation of wheat nutrients and maintains natural aroma.
