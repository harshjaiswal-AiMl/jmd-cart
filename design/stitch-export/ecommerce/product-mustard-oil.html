Traditional Cold-Pressed Kachi Ghani Process

Fortune Kachi Ghani Pure Mustard Oil is crafted by crushing finest mustard seeds at room temperature using authentic wooden/cold press mechanisms to retain their natural pungency, volatile aromatic oils, and golden brilliance. Known locally across Bihar as "Khadisheel Teekha Tel", it adds unmatched flavor to Bihari mutton curry, litti chokha, raw mustard seed chutneys, and winter pickles.
